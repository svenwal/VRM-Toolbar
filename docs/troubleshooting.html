Troubleshooting & privacy

Common problems, and exactly what the app talks to.

Troubleshooting

“Not signed in” in the menu bar

Open Settings › Account and sign in. Check that the token was pasted without extra spaces.

No values in the list

Check that your installation is online in the VRM portal. Press Refresh in the menu bar menu.

Nothing is live (no ⚡)

The live stream needs an access token. Password sign-in only polls.

Password sign-in fails

Accounts with two-factor authentication must use an access token.

Keychain asks for my password constantly

Sign the app with a stable identity, see Build from source.

The widgets are not listed

Launch the app once from its final location, then reopen Edit Widgets. If they still do not show up, run pluginkit -a /Applications/VRMToolbar.app/Contents/PlugIns/VRMWidget.appex. Unsigned or ad-hoc signed builds may be rejected.

The menu bar item disappeared

Crowded menu bars (especially on notched Macs) hide overflow items. Remove other items or shorten labels.

Privacy

  • VRM Toolbar talks only to Victron’s services: the VRM API at vrmapi.victronenergy.com and the VRM MQTT broker at webmqtt*.victronenergy.com.
  • No analytics, telemetry or third-party servers.
  • Credentials live in your login Keychain. Display settings are stored locally.
