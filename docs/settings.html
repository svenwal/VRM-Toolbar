General settings

Refresh interval, launch at login, and Dock behaviour.

Refresh every

How often the VRM API is polled: 30 seconds, 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes. Streamed values update instantly regardless.

Launch at login

Registers VRM Toolbar as a login item so your readouts are there when you sit down.

Dock icon

The app runs as a menu bar accessory without a Dock icon. The icon appears only while at least one display targets the Dock. Clicking the Dock icon or relaunching the app opens Settings.

Overview page

The Overview shows a battery ring, a tile for each display and the time of the last update. Its colour (green above 60 %, amber above 30 %, red below) also tints the animated background across the settings window.
