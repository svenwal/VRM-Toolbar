Account & access token

Connect VRM Toolbar to your Victron VRM portal securely.

Access token (recommended)

An access token works with two-factor authentication and is required for the live stream.

  1. Sign in at vrm.victronenergy.com.
  2. Open Preferences › Integrations.
  3. Under Access tokens, create a new token and copy it.
  4. In VRM Toolbar choose Account › Access token, paste it and press Sign in.

Email & password

The alternative sign-in uses your VRM email and password. It does not work with accounts that have two-factor authentication, and Victron has deprecated password access for its APIs. Use a token when you can.

Where credentials are stored

Credentials are saved in your login Keychain under the service world.walther.vrm-toolbar. They are only sent to Victron’s servers. Sign out removes them from the Keychain.

Keychain promptsIf macOS asks for your password after every rebuild, the app is signed ad hoc. Sign with a stable identity, see Build from source.

Several installations

If your account has more than one installation, each display has an Installation picker, so you can mix values from a boat, a camper and a cabin.
